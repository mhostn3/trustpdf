Why TrustPDF

Runs on your device

Processing happens in your browser with open-source libraries. Close the tab and nothing remains.

Nothing to sign up for

No account, no email, no cookies. Not even a cookie banner, because there is nothing to accept.

Fast and free

No queues, no limits, no watermarks. Files are ready the moment the work is done.

Verifiable

The code is public under the AGPL v3. Read it, run it yourself, or check it on GitHub.

How it works

  1. Open a tool. The page loads open-source PDF libraries into your browser.
  2. Add your file. It is read straight from your disk into the page's memory, never sent over the internet.
  3. Save the result. Your browser builds the new file and saves it to your device. Close the tab and nothing is left behind.
