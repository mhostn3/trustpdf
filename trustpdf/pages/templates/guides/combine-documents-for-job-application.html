Many job portals ask for a single PDF, or let you upload only one or two files: your CV, cover letter, diplomas, certificates and references all have to fit. Here is how to put them together into one clean, small file that opens well on any computer, without uploading your personal documents to a converter site on the way.

1. Get every document into PDF form

  • CV and cover letter: export them from Word, Google Docs or Pages with Save as PDF or Download as PDF, rather than printing to PDF, so the text stays selectable.
  • Paper certificates: scan them, or photograph them flat in good light. Turn the photos into a PDF with Images to PDF, choosing A4 or US Letter as the page size so every page has the same format.
  • Pages you don't need: a ten-page transcript where only page 1 matters can be cut down with Extract PDF Pages.

2. Merge them in a sensible order

Open Merge PDF, add all the files and drag them into order. A common order is cover letter, CV, then certificates and references from most to least relevant, unless the job ad asks for something else. Click Merge PDFs and save.

If a certificate came out sideways, fix it with Rotate PDF Pages. For a long file, page numbers help the reader, especially if your cover letter refers to the attachments.

3. Check the size limit

Application portals often allow only a few megabytes per file. Scanned certificates are what make a file big. If yours is over the limit, use Compress PDF:

  • Try Lossless first. It changes nothing visible and keeps the text of your CV selectable.
  • If that is not enough, compress only the scanned certificates with Strong before merging, then merge them with your CV. That way the scans get smaller while your CV and letter stay sharp, searchable text.

4. Fix the title and author

A merged PDF takes its title and author from the first file. That might be the title of the template your CV started from, or a former employer's name if you made it on a work laptop. Open the PDF Metadata Editor, set the Title to something like "Ana Silva, Application for Product Designer", set the Author to your name, keep Also remove the embedded XMP metadata stream ticked, and save. The guide how to change a PDF's title and author has the details.

5. Name the file well

Recruiters download hundreds of files called "CV.pdf". Use your name and the role, for example Ana-Silva-Product-Designer.pdf, and avoid version numbers or words like "final".

A last check

  • Open the final file and scroll through every page: right order, no sideways pages, nothing cut off.
  • Press Ctrl+F (Cmd+F on a Mac) and search for a word in your CV. If it is found, the text is still selectable, which matters for portals that read your CV automatically.
  • Do not password-protect an application file; portals and recruiters usually cannot open protected PDFs.
