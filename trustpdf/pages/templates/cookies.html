Cookie Policy

The short version: TrustPDF sets no cookies on your visit.

Last updated: 8 November 2026

🍪 We do not use cookies

TrustPDF does not set cookies when you visit or use the tools, and does not store anything in your browser's local storage:

  • No cookies for advertising or tracking.
  • No cookies for preferences, sessions, or saved data.
  • No analytics cookies, not even anonymous ones.

Because no cookies are set, there is no cookie banner and nothing for you to accept or reject.

The only exception is a cookie you ask for yourself: if you choose not to be counted in the statistics, a strictly necessary cookie (tp_ignore, 10 years) remembers that choice on your device. Site staff also get the usual login cookies (sessionid, csrftoken) when they sign in to the administration area.

📊 Cookie-free analytics

TrustPDF counts page views and tool usage with its own first-party statistics, stored on our own server. They work without cookies or local storage, involve no analytics company, and cannot follow you across other websites. Details are in the Privacy Policy.

❌ No third-party ads or trackers

TrustPDF includes no advertising networks, tracking pixels, social media plugins, or third-party content delivery networks. Every script, stylesheet, and font is served from this site.

🔗 Other websites

Links to Proton (affiliate links), Buy Me a Coffee, GitHub, and other sites lead to services with their own cookie policies, which apply once you leave TrustPDF.
