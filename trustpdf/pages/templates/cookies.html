Cookie Policy

The short version: TrustPDF sets no cookies.

Last updated: 8 November 2026

🍪 We do not use cookies

TrustPDF does not set any cookies, and does not store anything in your browser's local storage:

  • No cookies for advertising or tracking.
  • No cookies for preferences, sessions, or saved data.
  • No analytics cookies, not even anonymous ones.

Because no cookies are set, there is no cookie banner and nothing for you to accept or reject.

📊 Cookie-free analytics

We use Umami Cloud to count page views and tool usage. It works without cookies, local storage, or fingerprinting, and cannot follow you across sites or sessions. Details are in the Privacy Policy.

❌ No third-party ads or trackers

TrustPDF includes no advertising networks, tracking pixels, social media plugins, or third-party content delivery networks. Every script, stylesheet, and font is served from this site.

🔗 Other websites

Links to Proton (affiliate links), Buy Me a Coffee, GitHub, and other sites lead to services with their own cookie policies, which apply once you leave TrustPDF.
