Privacy Policy

What TrustPDF does, and does not do, with your files and your visit.

Last updated: 8 November 2026

🔐 Your files never leave your device

Every tool on this site (merge, split, rotate, reorder, remove pages, metadata viewer and editor, images to PDF) runs entirely inside your browser using open-source JavaScript libraries (pdf-lib and PDF.js).

  • No file is uploaded, transmitted, or stored on our servers.
  • We never see your files, their contents, or their names.
  • Our server sends every page with a Content-Security-Policy header that forbids scripts from sending data anywhere other than this site and the analytics endpoint described below. You can verify this in your browser's developer tools.
  • When you close the page, nothing remains: files are held only in your browser's memory.

✅ No accounts, no personal data

  • There are no user accounts, logins, or registration forms.
  • We do not collect, store, sell, or share any personal information.
  • We do not set cookies and do not use local storage. See the Cookie Policy.
  • All CSS, fonts, and JavaScript are hosted on our own server, so no content delivery network or other third party sees your visit.

📊 Anonymous page statistics

We use Umami Cloud, a privacy-focused analytics service, to count page views and which tools are used (for example, how many times the "Merge PDFs" button is clicked). This helps us decide what to improve.

  • Umami uses no cookies and no local storage.
  • It records the page URL, referrer, browser type, device type, and country, derived from your IP address, which is not stored.
  • It does not record anything about your files, not even their names or sizes.
  • It cannot track you across other websites or over time.
  • Umami respects the browser "Do Not Track" setting and is blocked by most content blockers; the site works exactly the same without it.

To load the analytics script, your browser connects to cloud.umami.is, and the statistics are sent to api-gateway.umami.dev. These are the only third-party connections the site makes. Umami's own privacy policy is at umami.is/privacy.

🖥️ Server logs

Like almost every website, our web server keeps short-lived technical logs (requested page, time, IP address, browser type) for security and troubleshooting. They are not used to identify or profile visitors and are deleted automatically.

🔗 Affiliate links and donations

The Privacy Tools page and the small tip shown after each tool contain affiliate links to Proton. If you buy a Proton plan through one of them, TrustPDF receives a commission at no extra cost to you. Following a link takes you to Proton's site, which has its own privacy policy. The "Buy me a coffee" buttons lead to buymeacoffee.com, which is also independent of us. We receive no personal data from either.

🧒 Children

TrustPDF collects no personal data from anyone, including children.

📝 Changes to this policy

If we change how the site works, we will update this page and the date at the top. The full history is public in the source code repository.
