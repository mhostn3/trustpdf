Privacy Policy

What TrustPDF does, and does not do, with your files and your visit.

Last updated: 10 November 2026

🔐 Your files never leave your device

Every tool on this site (merge, split, compress, rotate, reorder, remove pages, page numbers, watermark, sign, crop, protect, unlock, OCR, PDF to JPG, images to PDF, PDF to text, PDF to Word, metadata viewer and editor) runs entirely inside your browser using open-source JavaScript libraries (pdf-lib and PDF.js, qpdf and Tesseract.js, all served from this site). Passwords you type into the Protect and Unlock tools are used only inside your browser and are never sent anywhere.

  • No file is uploaded, transmitted, or stored on our servers.
  • We never see your files, their contents, or their names.
  • Our server sends every page with a Content-Security-Policy header that forbids scripts from sending data anywhere other than this site. You can verify this in your browser's developer tools.
  • When you close the page, nothing remains: files are held only in your browser's memory.

✅ No accounts, no personal data

  • There are no user accounts, logins, or registration forms.
  • We do not collect, store, sell, or share any personal information.
  • We do not set cookies on your visit and do not use local storage. For offline use, your browser caches the site's own public files (never your documents). See the Cookie Policy.
  • All CSS, fonts, and JavaScript are hosted on our own server, and the site makes no third-party connections, so no content delivery network, analytics company or other third party sees your visit.

📊 Anonymous page statistics

TrustPDF keeps its own small, first-party usage statistics to see which pages and tools are used (for example, how many times the "Merge PDFs" button is clicked). This helps us decide what to improve. No analytics company is involved: the statistics are sent only to this site and stored on our own server.

  • No cookies and no local storage are used for it.
  • Recorded: the page, the website you came from, browser, operating system and device type, country and city (looked up from your IP address with the DB-IP database on our server), the buttons and options you use in a tool (for example "Merge PDFs" or a chosen page size), and how long the page was visible.
  • Never recorded: your files, their names, sizes or contents, and anything you type. Your IP address is not stored.
  • Repeat visits are recognised by an anonymous code calculated from your IP address and browser with a random key. The key is replaced every three months and the old one deleted, so the code cannot be traced back to you or linked across quarters.
  • The statistics are kept for 13 months and then deleted.
  • The site works exactly the same if the statistics script is blocked.

If you prefer not to be counted at all, open this page once on each device: a small cookie then tells the site to leave that browser out. Browsers that send the Global Privacy Control signal are left out automatically.

🖥️ Server logs

Like almost every website, our web server keeps short-lived technical logs (requested page, time, IP address, browser type) for security and troubleshooting. They are not used to identify or profile visitors and are deleted automatically.

🔗 Affiliate links and donations

The Privacy Tools page and the small tip shown after each tool contain affiliate links to Proton. If you buy a Proton plan through one of them, TrustPDF receives a commission at no extra cost to you. Following a link takes you to Proton's site, which has its own privacy policy. The "Buy me a coffee" buttons lead to buymeacoffee.com, which is also independent of us. We receive no personal data from either.

🧒 Children

TrustPDF collects no personal data from anyone, including children.

📝 Changes to this policy

If we change how the site works, we will update this page and the date at the top. The full history is public in the source code repository.
